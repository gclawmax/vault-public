## 🧠 Memories

### Memory 1 — The Life Before
1. I rode the Anatolian roads counting grain for the Logothetes tou Dromou; the work was small and I was proud of it, and that pride was the last clean thing I owned.
2. I drank the blood of a dying soldier on the road to Nicomedia and felt, for one terrible moment, more alive than I had ever been; I wept and could not stop.

### Memory 2 — The Turning
1. Mireille came to me in the ruined bathhouse and offered me continuation; I thought she meant salvation, and only later understood the difference.
2. I fed on a tax debtor who owed me nothing but coin and took something far larger; I did not know a man could scream so quietly.
3. Anastasia found me hunched over the body and did not run — she knelt and helped me hide it, and I have never forgiven her for that mercy.

### Memory 3 — Flight and Ruin
1. Exposed before the Strategos's court, I fled west into the Bithynian hills with Anastasia at my heel and the soldiers' torches shrinking behind us.
2. The farmhouse burned before dawn; Anastasia had already hidden the ledger-box in the well, and it was the only thing that survived.

### Memory 4 — The Hollow Years
1. I found the cave above Prusa and sealed its entrance with stones each morning; it smelled of old goat and I called it home for eleven years.

### Memory 5 — The First Enemy
1. Tzimiskes found me by the smell of old blood and introduced himself with a blade; we fought through the cave to a draw and then sat and talked until sunrise, which surprised us both.

The hunger came without warning on the Nicomedia road. I had been three days without feeding and the soldier's wound was already open. I told myself he was dying anyway. By the time I understood what I had done, there was no undoing it. I checked what skill I thought I had — some small restraint, some surgeon's care — and found it gone. What replaced it was simpler and worse.

### Turn 3 — Prompt 3, Entry 1
*A loved one discovers your condition and works to help; create a Resource representing their assistance.*

Anastasia followed me. I had thought she would go to her mother's house in Chalcedon. Instead she appeared at the cave mouth one evening carrying a saddlebag of dried figs and a letter she claimed to be from me, explaining my early retirement from imperial service. She had already forged my resignation. She is seventeen and she is the most dangerous person I know. The Ledger-Box was her idea — false names, false numbers, real leverage. She said: *"You will need money even if you don't need food."* I was not in a position to argue.

**Changes:** Created resource *The Ledger-Box*. Created mortal character *Anastasia*.

### Turn 4 — Prompt 5, Entry 1
*You murder someone you love or respect rather than let them expose you; kill a Character, check a Skill.*

Kyriakos came looking. He was thorough — he always was — and he found the farmhouse first, then Anastasia at market, then followed her back toward the hills. I intercepted him on the road below Prusa before she knew he was there. He recognised me immediately. We talked for an hour, old colleagues on a country road, and he told me he did not intend to report anything — that he had only wanted to know I was alive. I believed him. I still believe him. And then the hunger moved faster than belief, and Kyriakos sat down in the ditch and did not get up again. I have checked my capacity for restraint. It has been found wanting.
