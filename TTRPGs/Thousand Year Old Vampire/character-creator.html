Character Creation

Begin your immortal chronicle. Follow each step in order.

Step 1: Create Your Mortal Self

Define who you were before the curse

Imagine a person in the distant past — a Roman emperor, a Mesopotamian midwife, a French knight. Write one Experience encapsulating their history. This is a broad summary of their mortal life, not a single event.

Step 2: Mortal Characters

The people who shaped your mortal life

Create at least three Characters: relatives, friends, lovers, enemies, mentors, or debtors. Add them one at a time with a short description.

Step 3: Skills

What your vampire can do

Give your vampire three Skills that fit their mortal life and historical context. Examples: Swordplay, Relaxing Banter, Operate Heavy Machinery, or evocative phrases like "I Do Not Blink The Sand Away".

Step 4: Resources

What your vampire possesses

Give your vampire three Resources: assets, structures, or items of value. Size doesn't matter — an obsidian knife or a fleet of warships both work. Examples: A lucky penny, Castle Umbrecht, A business empire, A carboy of acid.

Step 5: Three More Experiences

Memories that define you

Create three Experiences, one for each of Memories 2–4. Each should combine two of your existing traits (a Character with a Skill, a Resource with a Skill, etc.).
